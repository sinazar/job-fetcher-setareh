We’re looking for a Client Operations Specialist to own the end-to-end rollout and quality assurance of our direct client campaigns at Leads.io.

From pre-launch preparation to live campaign management and post-campaign checks, you’ll ensure everything runs smoothly, accurately and to the highest standard.

Core responsibilities:

  • Building the client relationship during setting up new campaigns and ongoing campaigns.
  • Responsible for data input and aligning all the necessary systems in setting-up a new campaign.
  • Making sure to administrate all important details about the clients (i.e. invoicing).
  • Aligning client request to internal team as well as providing answers to both sides that are important to run a successful campaign.
  • Overlooking the big picture of running campaigns and managing it in a way that is timely, efficient and profitable for the company.
  • Responsible for arranging the post-campaign reporting for our Business Developers and
  • Marketing teams and also help provide the client with important insights/learnings for future campaigns.

Requirements

Role Specific Competencies:

  • You enjoy working with clients
  • Project management
  • Attention to Detail
  • Customer Service
  • Technically savvy
  • Time Management

One of Leads.io's cultural values is Thrive as a team, and with this in mind, we are committed to building teams that are as diverse as our clients in an inclusive way. Leads.io is looking for the best and most motivated talent and welcomes all applicants. Benefits

What do you get?

  • 20 vacation days per year
  • Hybrid working
  • The opportunity to develop new technical skills by working with channels such as Meta
  • Working in an open culture with plenty of room for personal initiative and growth
  • Knowledge-sharing sessions organized several times a year by internal and external experts
  • Amazing Friday Lunches
  • MacBook Pro, Logitech MX, or Apple Magic Mouse/Keyboard
  • The opportunity to work fully remotely from your dream location for up to one month per year
